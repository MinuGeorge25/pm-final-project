BU Pay & Credit roadmap

Riverty's first consumer card in Germany, from a friends-and-family test launch to a launch recommendation. Every card is scored for value and effort against the persona, the primary metric and the guardrail below.

Team of 5 (assumed)
1 PM, 3 engineers, 1 designer

Persona

Lena, 32, Cologne, a salaried heavy BNPL user with no real cushion. She wants money to leave her account only after she has decided to keep an item.

Primary metric

Month-2 activity of newly activated cards, from about 54% (51.0% and 57.1%) to 62%. The target and the 150-card minimum per cohort are assumptions.

Moment of misery

She can't see in one place what she owes on the card and on her pay later orders, and two reminders for different amounts arrive in one week.

Guardrail

Partner merchant share of card volume stays at or above 58.8%, now 63.1% in Month 4. A drop is the warning.
